Record my voice

The portfolio answers questions out loud. Record each answer in your own voice and the site will play your recording instead of the computer voice. Anything you skip keeps the computer voice.

1. Pick where recordings are saved

Click the button and choose the folder portfolio/assets/voice. Recordings and the list file manifest.json are saved there automatically, one by one.

No folder chosen yet. Without it you can still download each recording and copy it in by hand.

This browser cannot save to a folder. Use Chrome or Edge, or download each recording.

2. Record

  1. Find a quiet room and sit about a hand's length from the microphone.
  2. Read the line in your own words if you like. Keep it about the same length.
  3. Press Record, speak, press Stop, then Play to check. Re-record as often as you want.
0 of 0 recorded

3. Finish

Reload the portfolio (http://localhost:8000), tap the mic, ask a question. If you recorded that answer, you will hear yourself and see a "my voice" tag in the conversation.
