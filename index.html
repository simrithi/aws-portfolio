Projects

Dry

Varshik, a street-level flood-risk map

A vision pipeline on Amazon SageMaker (SegFormer, Depth Anything V2, YOLOv8) scores 60 m road cells for waterlogging risk from dashcam frames, terrain and rainfall. Amazon Bedrock writes the plain-language briefs. React and MapLibre, served from S3 and CloudFront.

Top-risk cells matched Delhi's official flood hotspots. Results are preliminary.

Oversell-proof flash-sale inventory

Redis Lua scripts make check-and-decrement a single atomic step, so two buyers can never claim the last unit. A virtual waiting room, token-bucket rate limiting behind Nginx and RabbitMQ workers release unpaid stock.

The risk labels on this page follow the same idea. Anything below the waterline is high risk. Drag the gauge to move it.
